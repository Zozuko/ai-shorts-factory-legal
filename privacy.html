Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

AI Shorts Factory Desktop respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the service.

2. Information We Collect

AI Shorts Factory Desktop may process information that you provide while using the service, including content created or uploaded by you and information required to connect supported social media accounts.

3. Social Media Account Information

When you connect a supported social media platform, AI Shorts Factory Desktop may receive account information and authorization data required to provide the requested publishing functionality.

Access tokens and other sensitive authorization credentials are stored securely and are not intentionally exposed through the application's user interface.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the service, create and process videos, authenticate connected services, and perform actions requested by the user.

5. Third-Party Services

AI Shorts Factory Desktop may interact with third-party services such as TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook when you choose to connect and use those services. Their respective privacy policies apply to their processing of your information.

To suggest topic ideas, the application reads public data from Wikipedia through the Wikimedia Foundation's public APIs: the week's most-read articles, their short descriptions, and "On this day" anniversaries. These requests need no account and contain no personal data; as Wikimedia requires, they identify the application by name and by this website's address. Wikimedia's processing of the requests is described in the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy. When a Pexels API key is configured, the application also searches Pexels for a suggested topic's name to check that stock footage of it exists; Pexels' own terms and privacy policy apply to those searches.

6. Google and YouTube API Services

AI Shorts Factory Desktop uses YouTube API Services. When you connect your YouTube channel, the application requests these Google OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used solely to upload the short-form videos you render in the application (final.mp4), with the title, description and tags you review, to your own YouTube channel, and only when you explicitly instruct the application to publish.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly, used only to read the name of the connected channel, so the application can show you which channel a video will be published to.

All OAuth 2.0 tokens and other user data received from Google are stored strictly locally on your desktop device, encrypted with Windows DPAPI (Data Protection API). AI Shorts Factory Desktop does not collect or store Google user data on any external server. Google user data is not sold, is not shared with or transferred to third parties, is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

AI Shorts Factory Desktop's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI Shorts Factory Desktop complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service. Google APIs are never used for AI-generated Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (AI NCII) or any sexually explicit, deceptive, or harmful content.

By using the YouTube integration of AI Shorts Factory Desktop, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's processing of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke AI Shorts Factory Desktop's access to your Google account at any time, either with the Disconnect button in the application (which revokes the authorization with Google and deletes the locally stored tokens) or on the Google Security Settings page.

7. Data Sharing

AI Shorts Factory Desktop does not sell your personal information. Information may be transmitted to connected third-party services when necessary to provide functionality that you explicitly request.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the service. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality or maintain the service. Users may disconnect supported social media accounts through the application's available controls.

10. Data Deletion

Because AI Shorts Factory Desktop stores your data on your own device, you control its deletion. Disconnecting a TikTok, YouTube or Facebook account in the application removes its stored credentials from your device. Deleting a project in the application, or its project folder on your device, removes its scripts, narration and videos. To ask for help with deleting your data, contact dvishuddhiya@yahoo.com. Videos you have already published remain on the platform you published them to and can be deleted there.

11. Your Choices

You may choose whether to connect your social media accounts and may disconnect them when supported by the service.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: dvishuddhiya@yahoo.com
